# Earth Connect — Concept Storyboard

**Purpose:** test whether developers want guests to bring objects into independently created 3D spaces. **Illustration, not a product demonstration.**

| Shot | Seconds | Image | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0–4 | A creator's own 3D café | Your world. Your rules. |
| 2 | 4–8 | A visitor approaches from another digital place | A visitor arrives from another world. |
| 3 | 8–13 | Visitor brings Mimi, a digital cat | Meet Mimi — from another world. |
| 4 | 13–18 | The host considers allowing Mimi into a guest area | Your world decides what enters. |
| 5 | 18–23 | Mimi appears as a permitted guest object | Visitors could bring something with them. |
| 6 | 23–27 | Closing research prompt | Would you use this in your Three.js world? |

At beginning or end display: **Concept only — no public SDK yet**.

Do not show fake real-time synchronization, automatic object migration, arbitrary collision or physics behavior as existing features. This story is a *future workflow hypothesis*.

## Deliverables
- `demo/storyboard.svg`: lightweight static storyboard
- `demo/concept-preview.mp4`: concept animation (planned asset; add only if correctly uploaded)
- `demo/frame-1.png` through `frame-4.png`: optional storyboard frames

## Test prompt
“Would this solve a problem in a 3D world you already run? What would stop you from integrating it?”
