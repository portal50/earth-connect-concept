# Community post — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

**Title:** Would you let visitors bring digital objects into your existing Three.js world?

Hi everyone,

I'm exploring **Earth Connect**, a concept associated with the Earth Online project.

Imagine you've built a 3D café or gallery with Three.js. A visitor arrives from another independently built world, bringing a digital cat called Mimi. Your scene stays under your control, and you decide whether the guest object may appear and what limited interactions are permitted.

This is **concept research**, not an available SDK. We are not claiming one-line support for arbitrary worlds, physics systems, or object migration.

If you already maintain a browser 3D project:
- Would this provide value beyond simply importing a 3D model?
- What problem in your existing project would it solve?
- Would you allow foreign objects in your scene? What would you prohibit?
- What integration effort would be acceptable?
- What existing tools solve this today?
- Would you consider testing a limited future prototype with an existing project?

Critical responses are especially useful. Thank you.
